## Content Opportunities

**1. Scale the Claude Code / AI systems formula**  
It's the highest-signal theme across both platforms. Next angles with the same formula: Cursor for marketers, .cursorrules-style briefs, n8n as a systems layer (not just an automation tool), Airtable as a context engine.

**2. Always pair YouTube uploads with LinkedIn posts**  
The May 13 Claude Code example demonstrates measurable amplification. Make this a default workflow: every YouTube upload gets a corresponding LinkedIn post on the same day.

**3. Build a recurring research series**  
Two data posts (AI FOMO Reddit analysis, job openings analysis) performed well when wrapped in narrative. "We analyzed [X] to understand [Y]" is a repeatable format that builds audience recognition and gives the content team a consistent production loop.

**4. Replace promotional posts with pre-launch insight posts**  
Instead of "prices increase May 31st," publish the insight that motivated the product. The CTA goes in the comments. Value does the selling.

**5. Increase LinkedIn cadence to 3×/week**  
Current 1.2×/week limits compounding. Target: 1 data/research post + 1 systems/how-to post + 1 opinion/trend post per week.

**6. Experiment with YouTube Shorts**  
Cut 60-second clips from the Claude Code and n8n webinars. These are already produced — Shorts require only editing, not new production. Even modest Shorts traction would give the algorithm a signal to push the long-form content.

**7. Stop cross-posting LinkedIn content to Instagram verbatim**  
Instagram needs Reels, visual-first carousels, and shortened captions — not LinkedIn posts. Either adapt content properly for the platform or deprioritize it. Cross-posting identical content actively signals low effort to both the algorithm and the audience.
