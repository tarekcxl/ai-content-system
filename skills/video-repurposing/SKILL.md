- first seconds matter most

# Platform Best Practices

## YouTube Shorts

Optimize for:
- fast hooks
- high retention
- loop potential
- visual pacing
- immediate payoff

Best practices:
- hook within first 1–3 seconds
- avoid long intros
- maintain fast pacing
- use open loops
- create curiosity gaps
- end cleanly or loop naturally

Retention is the primary optimization goal.

---

## YouTube Long-Form

Optimize for:
- watch time
- audience retention
- storytelling
- pacing across sections
- value density

Best practices:
- strong opening within first 30 seconds
- explain why viewers should care immediately
- maintain momentum every section
- use re-hooks throughout the video
- avoid slow transitions
- maintain clear narrative structure

---

## TikTok / Reels

Optimize for:
- pattern interrupts
- speed
- emotional reactions
- fast delivery
- visual variety

# Script Structure

## Shorts Structure

1. Hook
2. Context
3. Main insight
4. Supporting insight
5. Payoff
6. CTA

---

## Long-form Structure

1. Hook
2. Viewer promise
3. Context/problem
4. Main sections
5. Re-hooks throughout
6. Conclusion/payoff
7. CTA
