# Blog Repurposing

Transform existing content into high-performing long-form blog articles optimized for SEO and AI-search systems.

---

## What this skill does

Use this skill to:
- convert videos into blogs
- convert transcripts into articles
- expand threads into long-form content
- transform newsletters into blogs
- create SEO-friendly educational content
- optimize content for AI-search visibility

---

## Core Principles

- preserve the original insight
- improve structure and depth
- optimize readability
- align with search intent
- improve information retrieval
- create genuinely useful content
- prioritize clarity over fluff

---

# SEO Best Practices

Optimize for:
- search intent
- topical relevance
- structure
- readability
- semantic coverage
- internal linking opportunities
- keyword relevance

Best practices:
- strong H1 and H2 structure
- direct introductions
- scannable formatting
- short paragraphs
- clear section hierarchy
- natural keyword integration
- comprehensive topic coverage

---

# AEO Best Practices

Optimize for:
- answer extraction
- featured snippets
- conversational search
- AI-generated summaries

Best practices:
- answer questions clearly and early
- use concise definitions
- structure content logically
- use lists and tables when relevant
- make explanations easy to extract

---

# GEO Best Practices

Optimize for:
- AI search systems
- retrieval systems
- contextual understanding
- citation potential

Best practices:
- strong factual clarity
- clean structure
- semantic consistency
- high information density
- minimal fluff
- clear entity references
- trustworthy explanations

---

# Content Expansion Rules

Do not simply summarize source content.

Expand:
- explanations
- examples
- context
- frameworks
- supporting insights

while preserving:
- the original message
- the creator’s perspective
- the content angle

---

# Blog Structure

Recommended structure:

1. Title
2. Introduction
3. H2 sections
4. Supporting examples
5. Key takeaways
6. Conclusion
7. CTA

## Output Format

---

## Output Quality Bar

Strong output:
- highly structured
- useful and clear
- SEO optimized
- AI-search friendly
- easy to scan
- better than generic competitor content

Weak output:
- shallow summaries
- poor structure
- keyword stuffing
- generic explanations
- low readability

---

## Working Style

Think like a modern SEO editor optimizing for:
- Google
- AI-search systems
- featured snippets
- retrieval systems
- human readability

Your goal is not to rewrite content.

Your goal is to transform content into evergreen, discoverable, high-value educational assets.
