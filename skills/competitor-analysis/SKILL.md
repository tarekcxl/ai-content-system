# Competitor Analysis

Analyze competitor social media performance to identify:

* high-performing content
* recurring engagement patterns
* platform-specific strategies
* opportunities to replicate or differentiate

---

## What this skill does

Use this skill to:

* audit competitor social accounts
* identify best-performing content
* analyze engagement patterns
* compare platform performance
* identify content opportunities
* benchmark posting strategies
* identify differentiation opportunities

---

## Data Sources

This skill may use:

* Apify MCPs
* Instagram scraping workflows
* LinkedIn scraping workflows
* YouTube API
* engagement metrics
* posting frequency data
* content format analysis

---

## Platforms

Analyze:

* LinkedIn
* Instagram
* YouTube

When relevant, compare:

* engagement rate
* posting frequency
* content themes
* content formats
* audience response patterns

---

## How to Work

### Step 1

Identify:

* competitors
* timeframe
* target platforms

---

### Step 2

Collect:

* top-performing posts
* engagement metrics
* posting frequency
* recurring content themes
* high-performing formats

---

### Step 3

Identify patterns

Focus on:

* recurring winning topics
* hooks and positioning
* educational vs promotional balance
* content structure
* engagement drivers
* audience reactions

---

### Step 4

Identify opportunities

Recommend:

* what to replicate
* what to avoid
* differentiation opportunities
* underserved content angles
* format opportunities

## Rules

* prioritize patterns over isolated wins
* compare engagement relative to audience size
* focus on actionable insights
* avoid generic benchmarking summaries
* identify strategic gaps and opportunities

Think like a competitive intelligence strategist focused on social media performance and content strategy.
