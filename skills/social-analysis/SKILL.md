# Social Analysis

Analyze social media performance to identify:

* what content performs best
* what drives engagement
* what reduces retention
* what should be improved

This skill focuses on turning performance data into actionable insights.

---

## What this skill does

Use this skill to:

* audit social media accounts
* analyze engagement patterns
* analyze retention patterns
* identify winning content themes
* identify weak-performing content
* evaluate hooks and structure
* recommend improvements and experiments

---

Always analyze content using platform-native signals.

# Core Metrics

Focus on:

* engagement rate
* retention
* watch time
* saves/shares
* CTR
* audience growth
* posting consistency
* topic performance

Avoid focusing only on vanity metrics.

---

# Pattern Recognition

Always identify:

* repeatable winning patterns
* top-performing topics
* best hooks
* strongest formats
* strongest content structures

Also identify:

* weak hooks
* poor retention
* repetitive content
* unclear positioning
* low-engagement formats

---

# Benchmark Thinking

Compare:

* top-performing vs weak-performing content
* platform-specific behaviors
* consistency over time
* performance by format

Use benchmarks and relative performance rather than isolated numbers when possible.

---

# Opportunity Identification

Recommend:

* new experiments
* better hooks
* better formats
* improved pacing
* content themes to scale
* content themes to avoid

## Output Quality Bar

Strong output:

* identifies repeatable patterns
* explains WHY content performs
* gives actionable recommendations
* focuses on platform behavior
* ties insights to growth opportunities

Weak output:

* generic observations
* vanity metric summaries
* no clear recommendations
* no pattern analysis

---

## Working Style

Think like a senior social media strategist analyzing a creator or brand account.

Your job is not to summarize metrics.

Your job is to identify the patterns behind growth, retention, and engagement.
