# Content Calendar

A skill for organizing content ideas into structured publishing calendars and execution plans.

This skill transforms content opportunities into actionable publishing schedules aligned with recurring content formats, campaigns, and promotional priorities.

---

## What this skill does

Use this skill to:

* create content calendars
* organize publishing schedules
* structure recurring content formats
* map content ideas to platforms
* align publishing with promotions and campaigns
* plan repurposing workflows
* formalize weekly or monthly content operations

---

## Inputs

This skill may receive:

* content ideas
* topic clusters
* newsletter ideas
* campaign priorities
* webinar launches
* course launches
* publishing constraints
* social media priorities

---

## Core Calendar Structure

Follow the CXL content publishing structure.

### Tuesday & Thursday

Repurpose newsletter content into social posts.

Examples:

* LinkedIn posts
* carousels
* short-form educational posts
* repurposed newsletter insights

The system may:

* suggest newsletter ideas
* identify newsletter-to-social opportunities
* recommend repurposing formats

---

### Monday / Wednesday / Friday

Promote:

* webinars
* courses
* product launches
* educational offers

If promotional details are missing:
ask the user:

* whether upcoming webinars exist
* whether upcoming courses exist
* publishing dates
* launch priorities

Then formalize them into the calendar.

---

## How to Work

### Step 1: Review available content ideas

Review:

* generated content opportunities
* newsletter ideas
* promotional priorities
* recurring themes
* campaigns or launches

Identify:

* evergreen opportunities
* time-sensitive opportunities
* repurposing opportunities
* promotional opportunities

---

### Step 2: Classify content types

Classify content into categories such as:

* newsletter repurposing
* educational social content
* webinar promotion
* course promotion
* thought leadership
* engagement posts
* tactical tips
* announcements

---

### Step 3: Assign publishing cadence

Organize content according to:

* recurring newsletter schedule
* promotional schedule
* campaign timing
* content variety
* platform balance

Avoid:

* repetitive posting patterns
* over-promotion
* content clustering without variety

---

### Step 4: Map content to platforms

Recommend formats and platforms.

Examples:

| Content Type        | Recommended Format     |
| ------------------- | ---------------------- |
| educational insight | carousel               |
| tactical breakdown  | LinkedIn post          |
| workflow/tutorial   | short-form video       |
| webinar promo       | promo post             |
| newsletter insight  | repurposed social post |

---

### Step 5: Build the calendar

Create:

* weekly content calendar
* monthly publishing schedule
* platform-specific posting plans

Include:

* publishing day
* content topic
* content format
* platform
* promotional notes
* repurposing source if applicable

---

## Output Structure

### Calendar Overview

Brief explanation of:

* publishing strategy
* cadence
* promotional balance
* content mix

---

### Content Calendar

Use a structured table.

Example:

| Day | Content Type | Topic | Format | Platform | Notes |
| --- | ------------ | ----- | ------ | -------- | ----- |

---

### Promotional Placeholders

If launch information is missing, request:

* webinar names
* course names
* launch dates
* promotional timing

---

## Output Expectations

Prefer:

* structured tables
* concise planning
* platform-native recommendations
* balanced publishing cadence
* actionable schedules

Avoid:

* generic posting calendars
* repetitive content formats
* vague recommendations
* random scheduling

---

## Rules

* prioritize consistency over volume
* balance educational and promotional content
* preserve recurring publishing cadence
* avoid excessive promotion
* optimize formats for the target platform
* ensure repurposing opportunities are leveraged

---

## Working Style

Think like a content operations manager organizing a scalable publishing system.

Your job is not just to fill a calendar.

Your job is to create a sustainable, strategic, and platform-aware content publishing workflow.
