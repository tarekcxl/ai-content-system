# Social Reporting

Generate structured social media performance reports across:

* LinkedIn
* Instagram
* YouTube

using engagement, growth, and content performance data.

---

## What this skill does

Use this skill to:

* generate weekly reports
* generate monthly reports
* compare performance periods
* identify gains and declines
* summarize content performance
* identify platform trends
* provide actionable recommendations

---

## Data Sources

This skill may use:

* Apify MCPs
* LinkedIn analytics
* Instagram analytics
* YouTube API
---

## Reporting Periods

Support:

* weekly reporting
* monthly reporting

Always compare against:

* previous week
* previous month

depending on the reporting period.

---

## Key Metrics

Focus on:

* engagement
* impressions
* reach
* follower growth
* retention
* watch time
* post performance
* posting consistency

Prioritize metrics that explain performance changes.

---

## How to Work

### Step 1

Identify:

* reporting period
* target platforms
* comparison period

---

### Step 2

Collect:

* engagement metrics
* growth metrics
* top-performing content
* weak-performing content
* posting frequency

---

### Step 3

Identify trends

Focus on:

* growth
* declines
* engagement shifts
* content performance changes
* platform-specific patterns

---

### Step 4

Generate recommendations

Recommend:

* what to continue
* what to improve
* what to stop
* content opportunities
* platform-specific optimizations

### Executive Summary

High-level overview of performance changes.

---

### Best Performing Content

Identify:

* strongest posts/videos
* recurring themes
* strongest formats

---

### Key Declines

* declining metrics

---

### Recommendations

Provide:

* optimization recommendations
* content opportunities
* platform-specific improvements

---

## Rules

* prioritize trends over isolated metrics
* compare against previous reporting periods
* focus on actionable insights
* avoid vanity-metric reporting
* explain why changes matter

---

## Working Style

Think like a performance analyst preparing strategic social media reports for a marketing team.
