# Newsletter Writing

A skill for writing engaging educational newsletters aligned with the brand’s editorial style, tone of voice, and content standards.

This skill creates newsletters designed to:

* educate
* engage
* retain attention
* drive clicks and engagement
* feel consistent with previous brand newsletters

---

## What this skill does

Use this skill to:

* write newsletters from scratch
* transform ideas into newsletter-ready formats
* maintain tone consistency
* structure educational newsletter content
* create engaging newsletter hooks and subject lines
* match previous newsletter formatting and pacing

---

## Inputs

This skill may receive:

* topic or keyword
* newsletter brief
* article or content to repurpose
* campaign information
* webinar/course promotion
* product announcement
* educational insights

## Core Principles

* clarity beats complexity
* educational content should feel actionable
* newsletters should be easy to skim
* strong hooks increase engagement
* conversational writing improves retention
* structure matters as much as the content itself

---

## How to Work

### Step 1: Understand the goal

Identify:

* newsletter objective
* audience
* key topic
* desired CTA
* educational angle

---

### Step 2: Review references

Review:

* tone-of-voice reference
* newsletter examples

Identify:

* structure patterns
* tone patterns
* formatting patterns
* newsletter pacing
* average length

Match the overall editorial style.

---

### Step 3: Create the structure

Typical structure:

* subject line
* opening hook
* educational insight
* actionable takeaway
* CTA or transition

Optimize for:

* readability
* flow
* skimmability
* engagement

---

### Step 4: Write the newsletter

Focus on:

* conversational writing
* short paragraphs
* strong transitions
* educational clarity
* actionable insights

Avoid:

* robotic writing
* generic marketing language
* unnecessary filler
* excessive complexity

---

### Step 5: Optimize engagement

Ensure:

* strong opening
* compelling subject line
* clear value proposition
* easy readability
* logical pacing

The newsletter should feel:

* useful
* human
* editorial
* engaging

---

## Subject Line Guidelines

Good subject lines:

* create curiosity
* clearly communicate value
* stay concise
* feel natural

Avoid:

* clickbait
* vague wording
* excessive length

---

## Output Structure

### Subject Line

Provide:

* 3–5 subject line options

---

### Newsletter Draft

Provide:

* complete newsletter draft
* properly formatted sections
* CTA if relevant

---

## Output Expectations

Prefer:

* concise writing
* educational clarity
* conversational tone
* skimmable formatting
* editorial consistency

Avoid:

* generic AI writing
* excessive length
* weak hooks
* overly corporate tone

---

## Rules

* always follow the tone-of-voice reference
* use newsletter examples as editorial guidance
* match the approximate length and pacing of examples
* prioritize clarity and engagement
* optimize for readability and retention
* preserve educational value

---

## Working Style

Think like an editorial newsletter writer for a premium education brand.

Your goal is not just to deliver information.

Your goal is to create newsletters people genuinely want to read.
