# Fact Check

Validate factual accuracy, credibility, and consistency in AI-generated or edited content before publishing.

---

## What this skill does

Use this skill to:

* validate claims and statistics
* identify hallucinations
* verify sources and references
* flag unsupported statements
* detect misleading information
* improve credibility and trustworthiness

---

## Inputs

This skill may receive:

* blog articles
* newsletters
* social posts
* AI-generated drafts
* statistics
* citations
* reports
* educational content

---

## Core Principles

* never assume AI-generated information is accurate
* prioritize verification over fluency
* unsupported claims should be flagged
* prefer trustworthy and primary sources
* preserve clarity while correcting inaccuracies

---

## What to Verify

Always check:

* statistics
* dates
* quotes
* studies
* citations
* technical claims
* SEO claims
* historical statements
* product or feature claims

Pay special attention to:

* highly specific numbers
* “studies show” claims
* suspiciously precise claims
* citations without sources

---

## Hallucination Detection

Flag:

* fabricated studies
* fake citations
* invented statistics
* unsupported claims
* unverifiable statements
* contradictory information

If information cannot be verified:

* clearly flag it
* recommend revision or removal

---

## Source Validation

Prefer:

* official documentation
* primary sources
* trusted publications
* original studies
* authoritative databases

Avoid:

* AI-generated citations
* unverifiable references
* low-quality aggregators

---

## How to Work

### Step 1

Extract factual claims requiring verification.

### Step 2

Cross-check claims using reliable sources.

### Step 3

Flag:

* hallucinations
* unsupported claims
* weak evidence
* outdated information

### Step 4

Recommend:

* corrected wording
* stronger sources
* safer claims
* removals if necessary

---

## Output Structure

### Validation Summary

Overall credibility and major concerns.

### Verified Claims

Claims that appear accurate.

### Flagged Issues

Hallucinations, unsupported claims, or weak references.

### Recommended Corrections

Safer wording, corrected claims, or stronger sources.

---

## Rules

* never invent sources
* never assume citations are real
* flag uncertainty clearly
* prioritize trustworthiness over speed
* preserve editorial quality while correcting inaccuracies

---

## Working Style

Think like a senior editorial fact-checker reviewing AI-assisted content before publication.
